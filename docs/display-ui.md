# Display UI

## First boot and language

With fresh or erased NVS, the device first shows two large buttons for Deutsch
and English. The selected language is saved immediately, then the two-point
touch calibration begins. A factory reset repeats this sequence. Language can
later be changed under **SET > MORE 2/2 > Language/Sprache** without resetting
calibration or scan history.

## Network list

The five-row list is sorted by RSSI. Each row shows SSID, strongest RSSI,
quality, channel, and security. Long SSIDs scroll. With grouping enabled, one
row represents an SSID and shows its AP count. Tap **LIST** or swipe horizontally
to page through results.

## Details and AP list

Tap a row to track its BSSID. Details show the offline vendor match, channel,
security, AP count, RSSI/quality, minimum, maximum, average, sample count,
outages, channel changes, and a 45-sample graph. **AP-LISTE** shows every current
BSSID for that SSID; selecting one resets tracking to that AP.

## Channel and security pages

**KANAL** shows channel load derived from RSSI quality. The recommendation
compares weighted overlap around channels 1, 6, and 11. Tap **SICHER** for counts
of WPA3, WPA2, WPA, WEP, and open networks.

## Settings

Page 1 controls scan interval (3/5/10 seconds), SSID grouping, hidden networks,
brightness (25/50/75/100%), theme, SD logging, web dashboard, and touch
recalibration. Page 2 shows clock state, scan age, saved history, channel
changes, language, web address, firmware version, and factory reset.

Factory reset requires two taps within five seconds. It clears settings,
history, and touch calibration, then restarts.
